# Decision Report Format

Section layout for Step 6 of [`SKILL.md`](../SKILL.md). Read it before writing the report.

**Money in every section**: lead every amount with the user's currency, in tables and sentences alike, including prices quoted from foreign sources and costs mentioned in passing. Never write a foreign amount alone, even when quoting a source: write "the price rose from ₱2,800 to ₱3,900 (US\$50 to US\$70)", not "rose from US\$50 to US\$70". If no current exchange rate was found, say so once above the first table and mark converted amounts approximate.

## 1. Verdict

- **Verdict**: `[Buy | Buy With Conditions / Wait | Buy Alternative Instead | Rent / Borrow / Trial First | Keep Current / Repair | Do Not Buy]`
- **Bottom line**: 2 to 3 decisive sentences stating the verdict, the main money and practical reason, the total cost over the holding period, and the cost per use (or per hour) even if the user uses it half as much as they expect.

## 2. Cost Comparison

A side-by-side Markdown table comparing **This Item**, the **Best Alternative** (or a used/refurbished version), and the **Best Not-Buying Option** (keep, repair, or rent) across these rows: Upfront Cost, Hidden & Recurring Costs, Resale Value, Total Cost Over N Years, Cost Per Use (at half your expected use), and Main Trade-off.

## 3. What Could Go Wrong

- **Real-world problems**: what independent testers and long-term owner complaints show, with dated, session-verified links.
- **Add-ons and other uses for the money**: the extra items people usually end up buying, priced out, and what else the same money could do (pay down debt, build savings, fund a bigger goal).
- **If you regret this in a year**: the most likely reason, and how easy it is to undo (return window, restocking fees, how quickly it resells).

## 4. What Would Change the Verdict, and Next Steps

- **What would change the verdict**: exact numbers or conditions (for example, *"Switches from `Do Not Buy` to `Buy` if the price drops below [price] or you'd use it more than Y times a month"*).
- **Next steps**: if buying or trying, the most to pay, where and which version to buy, and a checklist to test it during the return window; if skipping or waiting, the exact price alert or trigger to set.

## 5. Save for Next Time (when applicable)

If the session revealed lasting buyer context (currency, budget limits, the value of the user's time, owned gear or subscriptions) or a final purchase decision, ask in one line whether to save it as the buyer profile for future decisions.
